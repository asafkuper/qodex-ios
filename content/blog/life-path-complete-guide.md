## What Is a Life Path Number?

Your Life Path Number is the most significant number in your numerology chart. Derived from your birth date, it reveals your life's purpose, natural talents, challenges, and the path you're meant to walk.

Think of it as your spiritual DNA—the essence of who you are at the deepest level.

---

## How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

### Method 1: The Traditional Way

1. Write down your full birth date (MM/DD/YYYY)
2. Reduce each component to a single digit:
   - Month: March = 3
   - Day: 15 → 1 + 5 = 6
   - Year: 1990 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
3. Add the results: 3 + 6 + 1 = 10
4. Reduce to single digit: 1 + 0 = **1**
