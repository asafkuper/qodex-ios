## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Send Times (EST)
- **Welcome emails**: Immediate
- **Newsletters**: Tuesday 10AM
- **Promotional**: Thursday 2PM
- **Urgent**: Any time

### Subject Line Tips
- Use emojis (✨🎉📱)
- Keep under 50 characters
- Personalize with first name
- Create curiosity

### Tone Guidelines
- Warm and personal
- Shani's voice (not corporate)
- Short paragraphs
- Clear CTAs

### Compliance
- Unsubscribe link in footer
- Physical address
- Honor unsubscribe immediately
