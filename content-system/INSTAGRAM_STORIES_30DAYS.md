## STORY DESIGN SPECS

**Visual Style:**
• Background: #0A0A0F (deep black)
• Text: White #FFFFFF
• Accents: Pink #FF00FF or Cyan #00FFFF
• Font: Heebo Bold (Hebrew headlines)

**Sizes:**
• Headlines: 48-60pt
• Body: 32-36pt
• Small text: 24pt

**Effects:**
• Glow on numbers (pink/cyan, 20px blur)
• Subtle grain overlay (15%)
• Logo watermark (bottom corner, small)

**Interactive Elements:**
• Place in center or bottom third
• Keep clear of text
• Use brand colors for poll options

## STORY PRODUCTION WORKFLOW

**Daily (10 minutes):**
1. Choose 3-5 stories from templates
2. Customize text for today's content
3. Add interactive elements
4. Post throughout day

**Tools:**
• Instagram native (quickest)
• Canva (for designed stories)
• CapCut (for video stories)

**Best Times to Post Stories:**
• 9 AM (morning scroll)
• 12 PM (lunch break)
• 3 PM (afternoon slump)
• 6 PM (commute/home)
• 9 PM (evening scroll)
