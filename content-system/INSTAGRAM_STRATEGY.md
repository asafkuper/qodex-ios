## 🎯 STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATIONS

### 1. CONTENT PILLARS (The 4 Qodes)

#### Qode 1: Daily Energy (40% of content)
**What:** Daily numerology forecasts (1-9)
**Format:** Reels 15-30 seconds
**Hook:** "Today's energy is [NUMBER] - here's what that means for you..."
**Visual:** Animated number with cosmic glow (like app design)

**Examples:**
- "יום 7 | Day of Wisdom - perfect for research and meditation"
- "יום 3 | Creative energy is HIGH today - start that project!"
- "יום 8 | Money moves only - financial decisions favored"

#### Qode 2: Celebrity Decoded (20% of content)
**What:** Decode famous personalities/companies
**Format:** Carousels (3-5 slides)
**Hook:** "The hidden numerology behind [NAME] will shock you..."
**Visual:** Split screen - photo left, numbers right

**Target List:**
- Israeli celebrities (Bar Refaeli, Gal Gadot, Netta)
- Global icons (Elon Musk, Beyoncé, Messi)
- Companies (Tesla, Apple, McDonald's)
- Historical figures (Einstein, Tesla, Cleopatra)

#### Qode 3: Personal Stories (20% of content)
**What:** Client testimonials + Shani's insights
**Format:** Reels with voiceover
**Hook:** "Her Life Path was 9 - here's why she felt lost until..."
**Visual:** Anonymous silhouette + text overlay

#### Qode 4: Educational (20% of content)
**What:** "How to" numerology content
**Format:** Carousels + Reels
**Hook:** "Stop calculating your Life Path wrong - do this instead..."
**Visual:** Step-by-step graphics
