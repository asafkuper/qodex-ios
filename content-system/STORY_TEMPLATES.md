## STORY HIGHLIGHTS ORGANIZATION

### Create These Highlights:

1. **״תחיל״ (Start Here)**
   - What is QodeX
   - How to calculate Life Path
   - First 3 stories for new followers

2. **״מדריכים״ (Guides)**
   - How-to carousels
   - Educational content
   - Myths debunked

3. **״תחזיות״ (Forecasts)**
   - Daily Qodes
   - Monthly forecasts
   - 2025 predictions

4. **״פיענוחים״ (Decodings)**
   - Celebrity numerology
   - Company codes
   - Historical figures

5. **״קהילה״ (Community)**
   - User testimonials
   - Q&A highlights

## STORY DESIGN SPECS

**Dimensions:** 1080 x 1920px (9:16)

**Fonts:**
- Headlines: Heebo Bold, 80pt
- Body: Assistant Regular, 48pt
- Small: Assistant Light, 32pt

**Colors:**
- Background: #0A0A0F
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Accent: #FF00FF (pink) or #00FFFF (cyan)
- Gold numbers: #FFD700

**Elements:**
- Subtle glow on numbers
- Clean lines/dividers
- Emoji for visual breaks
- Logo watermark (small, corner)

---

**Daily Story Count Target:** 5-8 stories per day

**Mix:**
- 2 Daily Qode/energy
- 1 Engagement (quiz/poll/question)
- 1 Value (fact/BTS/education)
- 1 Evening (reflection/CTA)
- Plus real-time responses to engagement
