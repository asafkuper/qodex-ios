## CANVA DESIGN SPECS FOR CAROUSELS

**Dimensions:** 1080 x 1350px (4:5 ratio - best for feed)

**Slide Structure:**
- Slide 1: Bold hook, minimal text
- Slides 2-4: Educational content, scannable
- Final slide: Clear CTA

**Colors:**
- Background: #0A0A0F (deep black)
- Text primary: #FFFFFF (white)
- Text accent: #FF00FF (hot pink) or #00FFFF (cyan)
- Highlight: #FFD700 (gold) for numbers

**Fonts (Hebrew):**
- Headlines: Heebo Bold or Rubik Bold
- Body: Open Sans Hebrew or Assistant
- Numbers: Large, bold, with glow effect

**Visual Elements:**
- Subtle grain texture overlay (20%)
- Glow effects on numbers (#FF00FF, 30px blur)
- Clean white lines/dividers
- Logo on every slide (bottom corner)

**Text Rules:**
- Maximum 5-6 lines per slide
- Large font size (minimum 32pt)
- High contrast (white on black)
- Emojis for visual breaks

## POSTING STRATEGY FOR CAROUSELS

**Best Days:** Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday
**Best Times:** 8-10 PM Israel time
**Frequency:** 2-3 carousels per week

**Mix:**
- 40% Educational (How-to, guides)
- 30% Celebrity decoded
- 20% Personal identification ("Signs you're...")
- 10% Trending topics (2025 forecast)

**Engagement Tactics:**
- Slide 1: Strong hook that creates curiosity gap
- Middle slides: Value that makes people save
- Last slide: Clear CTA + "שמור את הפוסט הזה"
