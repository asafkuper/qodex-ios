## 8. RETENTION STRATEGIES

### 8.1 Streak Mechanics

**Current Implementation:**
- Track consecutive days of app opens
- Milestones at 7, 30, 100, 365 days
- Streak freeze for premium users

**Enhancements:**
- **Streak themes**: Different visual themes per milestone
- **Streak recovery**: Gamified "save your streak" mini-game
- **Streak bets**: Wager streak points on maintaining streak
- **Social streaks**: Streaks with friends/matches

### 8.2 Notification Strategy

**Smart Notification Framework:**
| Trigger | Message | Timing | Target Open Rate |
|---------|---------|--------|------------------|
| Daily Qode | "Your daily wisdom awaits ☀️" | User's morning time | 65% |
| Streak risk | "Your 23-day streak ends in 2h!" | 10 PM (if no open) | 78% |
| Live session | "Shani goes live in 30 min" | 30 min before | 45% |
| Community | "Someone replied to your insight" | Real-time | 25% |
| Alignment | "Your Life Path aligns today ✨" | Morning | 55% |
| Re-engagement | "The universe missed you" | Day 3 inactive | 20% |

**Anti-Spam Rules:**
- Max 2 push notifications per day
- Never send between 10 PM - 7 AM (user timezone)
- Always provide value, never "come back"
- Learn from user behavior (ML optimization)
